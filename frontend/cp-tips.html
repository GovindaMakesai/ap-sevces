Tip

  1. CP Home is a testimony of a couple's love.
  2. To unlock CP Home you need 2,000 support points with someone or accept a CP invitation.
  3. When support points reach 6,000 and you are friends, you can send a CP invitation with a ring.
  4. Rings are purchased in the Store. If an invitation fails, the ring returns to your bag.
  5. An invitation is valid for 48 hours. If it expires, resend it.
  6. If rejected, you cannot invite that person again for 48 hours.
  7. Once in CP, tap the ring on CP Home to change it or take it off to break up.
  8. Couples can break up voluntarily or one person can force a break-up.
