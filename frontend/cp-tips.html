CP Instructions

  1. Love House is a testimony of a couple's love — your CP ring shows on your profile, Love House, and in party rooms when you sit together.
  2. To unlock Love House you need intimacy value ≥ 20,000 (2,000 support points) with someone, or accept a CP invitation.
  3. When intimacy reaches ≥ 50,000 and you are mutual friends, you can send a CP invitation with a ring you bought from the Store.
  4. Rings are purchased in Store → Rings. If an invitation is declined or expires, the ring returns to your bag.
  5. An invitation is valid for 48 hours. If it expires, send it again.
  6. If rejected, you cannot invite that person again for 48 hours.
  7. Once CP, tap your ring on Love House to change it or break up. Both partners see the same ring on profiles.
  8. Level up by sending gifts — open Level from Store or your profile menu for benefits.

Tip: Sit next to your partner in a party room to show your ring between your seats.
