CP Instructions

CP House

  1. CP House is a testimony of a couple's love. Your CP ring shows on your profile, CP House, and in party rooms when you sit together.
  2. Unlock CP House with intimacy ≥ 20,000, or accept a CP invitation.
  3. Send a CP invite when intimacy is ≥ 50,000, you are mutual friends, and you own a ring from Store → Rings.
  4. Rings are bought in the store. If an invite is declined or expires, the ring returns to your bag.
  5. Invites last 48 hours. If rejected, you cannot invite that person again for 48 hours.
  6. Tap the ring on CP House to change it or take it off (break up).

Break up & ring change

  1. Take off the ring: ask your partner to agree (free), pay 75,000 coins for an instant break, or use the inactive-partner option if they have not been active for 30 days.
  2. Change the ring: pick a ring you own — your partner must accept within 48 hours.
  3. Incoming requests appear on CP House with Accept / Decline buttons.

Level & rankings

  1. Level up by sending gifts. Tap the shield icon for Level benefits and perks.
  2. CP Rankings list couples by intimacy from gifts after becoming CP. 1 💎 = 1 💖
  3. Week ranking counts gifts in the last 7 days; Total ranking is all-time intimacy.
