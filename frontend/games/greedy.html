How to Play

1. Pick a chip amount (100 → 100K).

2. Tap foods on the wheel to place bets (shared with everyone in this room).

3. When the timer hits 0, the wheel spins for everyone — even if you did not bet.

4. Winning History shows what landed for the room from everyone’s rounds.

If you bet on the winning food, you win your bet × the multiplier.
