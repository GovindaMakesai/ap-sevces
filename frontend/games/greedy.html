How to Play

1. Pick a chip amount (100 → 100K).

2. Tap one or more foods on the wheel to place bets.

3. When the timer hits 0, the wheel spins and lands on a food.

4. If you bet on the winning food, you win your bet × the multiplier.

Higher multipliers (Pizza, Roast Chicken) hit less often.
