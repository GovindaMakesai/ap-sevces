How to play

  1. Choose a chip amount (100, 1K, 10K…).
  2. Tap one or more fruits to place bets before the center timer hits 0.
  3. When time is up, the light spins and lands on a winning fruit.
  4. If you bet on the winning fruit, you win: bet × multiplier.
  5. Higher multipliers (x45, x25…) are rarer. Rounds start automatically.
