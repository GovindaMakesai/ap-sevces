CP Full Guide

How to become CP

  1. When intimacy reaches 50,000, you can send a CP invitation.
  2. CP can only have one partner.
  3. Up to 3 CP invitations per day.

CP Intimacy Value

  1. Increase intimacy by chatting, calling, and giving gifts.
  2. 1 💎 = 1 💖 (lucky gifts use actual income).

CP House

  1. CP House is a testimony of a couple's love. Your CP ring shows on your profile, CP House, and in party rooms when you sit together.
  2. Unlock CP House with intimacy ≥ 20,000, or accept a CP invitation.
  3. Send a CP invite at intimacy ≥ 50,000 when you are mutual friends and own a ring from Store → Rings.
  4. Rings return to your bag if an invite is declined or expires.
  5. Invites last 48 hours. Rejected invites have a 48h cooldown.
  6. Tap your ring on CP House to change it or take it off (break up).

Break up & ring change

  1. Take off the ring: ask partner to agree (free), pay 75,000 coins for instant break, or use inactive-partner break after 30 days.
  2. Change the ring: choose a ring you own — partner must accept within 48 hours.

Level & rankings

  1. Level up by sending gifts. Open Level from the shield icon for personal benefits.
  2. CP Rankings rank couples by gift intimacy after becoming CP. 1 💎 = 1 💖

Remove CP

There are two ways to remove CP:

  • a. Free CP cancellation application requires the consent of both parties before CP can be canceled;
  • b. If you pay to cancel the CP, you cancel the CP immediately without the consent of the other party. A part of the Coins spent will be given to the other party as compensation for canceling the CP;
  • After CP is lifted, the intimacy value will be cleared and re-calculated;
