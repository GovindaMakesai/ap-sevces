Data Deletion Instructions

AP Services

If you have an account with AP Services and would like your data removed, you may request deletion by email or from the app.

How to request deletion

In the app: Open Me → Settings → Request account deletion.

By email: Send a message to privacy@ap-services.com from the email linked to your account (if applicable). Use the subject line Data deletion request and describe the account or login method you used (for example, Facebook login or phone number).

Processing time

We process verified deletion requests and remove associated data within 7 days of confirming your request, unless we must retain certain information by law (for example, payment or fraud-prevention records).

Questions

For other privacy questions, see our Privacy Policy or contact privacy@ap-services.com.
