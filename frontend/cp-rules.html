Remove CP

There are two ways to remove CP:

  • a. Free CP cancellation application requires the consent of both parties before CP can be canceled;
  • b. If you pay to cancel the CP, you cancel the CP immediately without the consent of the other party. A part of the Coins spent will be given to the other party as compensation for canceling the CP;
  • After CP is lifted, the intimacy value will be cleared and re-calculated;
