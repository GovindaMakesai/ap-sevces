How to invite

How to invite

A successful invitation is counted when a friend downloads the app, logs in, and completes registration for the first time through your shared link, or enters your ID in the “Inviter ID” field during registration. Invitees must be either new users (first-time registrants) or returning users (inactive for 90+ days). For new users, completing registration via your link is sufficient. For returning users, they must first log in to the app, then click your invitation link and complete the boost immediately to qualify as a valid invitation.

How to earn rewards

Face authentication only validates the invite — it does not give points. You receive 10,500 points only after your invited host has completed 2 hours of live broadcast (tap Receive). Extra points unlock from the tasks below. Note: 1. The host only counts 3 hours of live broadcast towards the total per day. 2. Invitees flagged as invalid will not contribute any points.
