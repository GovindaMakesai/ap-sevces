Host Policy & Guidelines

Official Host Policy and Live Streaming Guidelines

1. Host Eligibility

  • All Hosts must be 18 years of age or above.
  • Hosts must provide accurate personal information and complete the required verification process.
  • Using another person's identity, name, photograph, or account without authorization is strictly prohibited.

2. Live Streaming Guidelines

  • Hosts must maintain respectful, professional, and decent behavior during every live session.
  • Abusive language, harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or intentionally disturbing users is prohibited.
  • Nudity, sexually explicit content, or inappropriate activities are strictly prohibited.
  • Promoting or displaying violence, illegal activities, dangerous acts, or harmful behavior is not allowed.

3. Live Hours & Host Performance

  • Hosts are expected to complete their assigned or agreed live-streaming hours regularly.
  • Hosts should actively interact with viewers and provide quality entertainment.
  • Repeatedly leaving live sessions without a valid reason or failing to complete required hours may affect incentives, performance status, or Host eligibility.

4. Gifts, Coins & Rewards

  • Hosts must never force, pressure, threaten, or manipulate users into sending gifts or purchasing coins.
  • Hosts must not use their own or related accounts to create fake gifts, coins, viewers, or engagement.
  • Any attempt to manipulate the reward system may lead to an account review, suspension, or termination.

5. Fraud & Misconduct

  • Fake viewers, bots, fake engagement, scams, or any artificial activity are strictly prohibited.
  • Hosts must not make false financial promises or deceive users for personal benefit.
  • Any suspicious financial activity or misuse of the platform may result in an investigation.

6. User Privacy & Safety

  • Hosts must respect the privacy and safety of all users.
  • Sharing a user's personal information without permission is prohibited.
  • Hosts must not request or misuse passwords, OTPs, bank details, addresses, private photographs, or other sensitive information.
  • Hosts should not encourage users to move to external platforms for suspicious or unauthorized activities.

7. Account Security & Multiple Accounts

  • Hosts must keep their login credentials secure and must not share their account with another person.
  • Creating multiple fake accounts to obtain additional benefits is prohibited.
  • Allowing another person to operate a Host account may result in suspension or termination.

8. Professional Conduct

  • Hosts must treat viewers, other Hosts, agencies, and AP Live Service team members with respect.
  • Hosts must follow official instructions issued by AP Live Service or its authorized representatives.
  • Disputes should be resolved through official support channels and not through threats, abuse, or harassment.

9. Disciplinary Actions

  • Official Warning
  • Temporary Suspension
  • Live Streaming Restriction
  • Bonus or Incentive Reduction
  • Earnings Review or Temporary Hold
  • Host Account Suspension
  • Permanent Account Termination
  • The action taken will depend on the seriousness and frequency of the violation.

10. Zero-Tolerance Violations

  • Sexual or explicit content
  • Any violation involving child safety
  • Serious harassment, threats, or abusive behavior
  • Fraud, scams, or financial deception
  • Fake engagement or system manipulation
  • Illegal activities
  • Unauthorized misuse or sharing of private information
  • Attempts to compromise, attack, or damage AP Live Service systems

11. Investigation & Enforcement

  • AP Live Service reserves the right to review and investigate suspected violations of this policy.
  • During an investigation, AP Live Service may temporarily restrict a Host account, live access, bonuses, rewards, or earnings where appropriate.
  • Hosts may be required to cooperate with reasonable verification or investigation requests.

12. Policy Updates

  • AP Live Service may update, modify, or introduce new policies when required for platform safety, compliance, or service improvement.
  • Hosts are responsible for staying informed about the latest applicable rules.

13. Policy Acceptance

  • By joining and operating as a Host on AP Live Service, the Host confirms that they have read, understood, and agreed to follow this Host Policy and Guidelines.
  • Failure to comply with AP Live Service policies may result in warnings, restrictions, suspension, or permanent termination of the Host account.
