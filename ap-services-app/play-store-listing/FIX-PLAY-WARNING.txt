FIX THIS PLAY WARNING (it is usually correct)

Google flagged your listing because screenshots / feature graphic look like
marketing posters or stock photos — not the real app UI.

DO NOT argue and submit as-is if you uploaded:
- Star Host / Normal Host policy posters as phone screenshots
- AI / stock model banners as feature graphic
Those will often get rejected.

────────────────────────────────────────
What to upload instead
────────────────────────────────────────

1) Feature graphic (1024 × 500)
   Use: Desktop\AP-Live-Play-Listing\feature-graphic-1024x500.jpg
   (brand logo + AP LIVE SERVICE text — no stock photos)

2) Phone screenshots (2–8 images, 9:16 preferred)
   Take from the REAL app after login (phone or emulator):
   - Explore / Live grid
   - A live room (chat + gifts visible)
   - Party room
   - Profile / Me tab
   - Wallet or gifts panel
   - Rankings or Invite page
   - Streamer Center (optional)

   How on Android:
   Open the installed app → open each screen → volume-down + power
   (outside live rooms you can screenshot; live rooms are blocked on purpose)

3) Tablet screenshots
   Either remove 10-inch / 7-inch if not needed, or upload the same
   real in-app screens (not posters).

────────────────────────────────────────
Then in Play Console
────────────────────────────────────────
Store listings → Default store listing
→ Replace Feature graphic
→ Replace Phone screenshots (delete poster images first)
→ Save
→ Send for review

Name / short / full description you already set is fine for a live app.
